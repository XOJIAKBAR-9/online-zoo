PAY AND FEED

Your donation has an impact

Providing our animals with high-quality nutrition diets is one element of our mission. Proper care promotes the health of our animals and ensures they develop to their full potential. A healthy animal diet may include meat, grains, fresh fruits and vegetables. Nutritious food for our animals helps them to be happy and healthy. Please help us provide nutritious food for our animals.

02

Make a donation

You can donate through your credit card without any fees. It is easy and safe. We do not keep donor's personal information on an online network. Choose our support to give and we will direct it to more effective ways you can possibly help. Thank you for your wonderful support.

03

Bring your food charity — straight to your favourite pets.

After your donation, the animal receives its favorite foods. You can support your favorite animals or any animal you care about and make a real social impact. Never doubt that you can make a difference even if it's small.
