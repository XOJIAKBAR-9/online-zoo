MEET SOME OUR PETS

Get up close with our incredible animals and discover what makes each species unique. Learn about their habitats, behaviors, and the conservation efforts keeping them safe.

Lucas

Giant Panda

Native to central China, giant pandas have come to symbolize vulnerable species. These gentle giants feed primarily on bamboo and are beloved worldwide.

Andy

Madagascan Lemur

These iconic primates with striking black and white tails are found only in Madagascar. Known for their social behavior and unique vocalizations.

Glen

Gorilla in Congo

The largest living primate, gorillas are intelligent and peaceful herbivores. These magnificent creatures play a vital role in their forest ecosystems.

Mike

Chinese Crocodile

One of the rarest crocodile species, the Chinese crocodile is a living fossil from ancient times. These reptiles are critically endangered and heavily protected.

Sam & Lora

West End Bald Eagles

Majestic symbols of freedom and power, bald eagles are remarkable hunters with exceptional vision. These birds form lifelong pairs and build massive nests.

Liz

Australian Koala

Despite their common name, koalas are marsupials, not bears. These cuddly-looking creatures are specially adapted to digest eucalyptus leaves.

Shake

African Lion

The king of beasts, lions are the most social of all wild cats. These powerful predators live in prides and hunt cooperatively across African savannas.

Senja

Sumatran Tiger

The smallest tiger subspecies, Sumatran tigers are critically endangered. These solitary hunters are perfectly adapted to dense rainforest environments.
